## How did you come up with 'GuyZ3R0' alias?

Do I have any regrets or wish that things turned out different for me?

Life won't always be perfect, thats the same for me. Whatever happened, happened. I have no regrets but I do wish that I had taken better decisions. So far, I'm doing great and don't have any resentment towards myself for making the mistakes.

So here I am. A guy starting from zero. GuyZ3R0. ('3' and '0' instead of 'E' and 'O' feels cooler :p)
